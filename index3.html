Free Software

Programs you can run, study, share, and change — without asking permission. No trials, no accounts, no catch. Just the software, and who made it.

Notes

"Free" here means freedom, not price — most of this is also free of charge, but the point of each project is that you're permitted to run, inspect, modify, and redistribute it. Licenses are listed as shorthand; read the project's own license file before relying on the details.

Missing something obvious? That's likely — no list this size stays complete for long.
